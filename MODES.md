## 🌟 The big picture

There are two families of modes, and knowing which family you are in saves a lot of confusion.

**Wheel modes** change which wheel you spin and which special rules apply. There are five, and they run in a fixed order: **Normal, Mystery, Express, Final Spin, Bonus Round**. You move between them with the round button on the puzzle board.

**Puzzle modes** change how a single puzzle is loaded and played. There are four: a regular puzzle (the default), a **Toss-Up**, a **Triple Toss-Up**, and a **Prize Puzzle**. You pick one by typing a short code after the puzzle number when you click **Load Puzzle**.

The two families combine freely. For example, you could be in the Mystery wheel mode and load puzzle 6 as a Prize Puzzle, or sit in Normal mode and open a Toss-Up. The only mode that is its own thing is the Bonus Round, which has its own letter-picking controls.

## 🔀 How to switch modes

### Switching the wheel (Normal, Mystery, Express, Final Spin, Bonus)

On the puzzle board, next to the blue **Load Puzzle** tab, there is a small colored button that shows the current round's name. It starts as **Normal Round**. Each click moves to the next one:

> **Normal Round → Mystery Round → Express Round → Final Spin round → Bonus Round → back to Normal Round**

The button changes color and label as you go. (In the code and on the button itself the fourth stop is labeled **Fourth Round**; it is the Final Spin round, and a purple **FINAL SPIN** button appears on the board once you are there.)

Good to know:

- You can only go forward. To get back to an earlier wheel, keep clicking until you loop around to Normal.
- The **Wheel** button at the top of the board always opens the wheel for whichever round you are currently in.
- While the Bonus Round countdown is running, the round button is blocked and a message tells you to reset the timer first. Click the countdown circle again to reset it.
- Leaving the Bonus Round clears any bonus letters that were still waiting to be revealed.
- Switching rounds resets the letter rules, so the next player starts with a clean slate.

### Switching the puzzle type (Toss-Up, Triple Toss-Up, Prize Puzzle)

Click the blue **Load Puzzle** tab at the bottom left of the board. A box asks for the puzzle number. Add a code to the number:

| You type | You get |
|---|---|
| `3` | Puzzle 3, played as a normal puzzle |
| `3T` | Puzzle 3 as a **Toss-Up** |
| `3TTT` | A **Triple Toss-Up** starting at puzzle 3 (it will use 3, 4 and 5) |
| `3P` | Puzzle 3 as a **Prize Puzzle** |

The letters are not case sensitive, so `3t` works too. One combination is not supported: `P` together with `T`. If you type something the game cannot read, it asks again.

## 🎯 Normal Round

**What it is.** The classic game, and the mode you start in. It is what the show plays for the first rounds.

**What is on the wheel.** Dollar wedges (the default range is $500 to $900, and you can change it in Wheel Settings), two **Bankrupt** wedges, a **Lose a Turn** wedge, and one top-prize wedge worth **$2,500**. Optional extras you can switch on in Settings include Free Play, Wild Card, Gift Tag, a $5,000 Sliver, a $5 wedge, and the Million Dollar Wedge. See [Wheel items](#-wheel-items-free-play-wild-card-gift-tag-and-friends).

### How to run it

1. Click **Load Puzzle** and type a plain number such as `2`.
2. Click the **Wheel** button at the top of the board, then click the wheel to spin. After about four seconds it lands and the result shows. Click **Back** to return to the board.
3. The player calls a consonant. Click that letter in the letter selector.
   - If it is in the puzzle, the matching tiles turn blue. Click each blue tile to reveal its letter. (Turn off **Blue Tiles** in Settings if you want letters to appear instantly.)
   - Click the player's **round score** to add the spun value times the number of matches. The value panel shows the math, for example `$700 * 2 = $1,400`.
   - If the letter is not in the puzzle, a buzzer plays and the turn passes.
4. To buy a vowel, click the small **orange circle** to the left of that player's round score (it costs $250 by default), then click the vowel.
5. When someone is ready to solve, click the left tab, which now says **Solve**.
6. Click the teal **Transfer Totals** arrow by the winner's totals score to bank the round. Then click **Load Puzzle** for the next one.

### What the wedges do

| Wedge | What happens | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| A dollar amount | The player calls one consonant for that amount per match. | Read the amount, let them pick, click their round score to add it. |
| **Bankrupt** | The player loses their round score and the turn. The turn passes automatically if the turn tracker is on. | Click the small **X** next to their round score to zero it. |
| **Lose a Turn** | No letter is allowed and the turn passes automatically. | Just let it pass. |
| **Free Play, Wild Card, Gift Tag, Million Dollar Wedge** | See [Wheel items](#-wheel-items-free-play-wild-card-gift-tag-and-friends). | |

### The letter rules the game enforces

The game follows the show's rules, and tells you why when something is not allowed.

- A spin that lands on a value allows exactly **one consonant**.
- A vowel cannot be called right after a spin. It has to be **bought** first, and one purchase buys one vowel.
- A letter that is not in the puzzle ends the turn.
- A player needs at least the vowel price in their round score to buy a vowel.
- With the turn tracker on, only the player whose turn it is can buy a vowel.

### What to say

> "Good evening, and welcome to *Wheel of Fortune*! Our category is... **Food and Drink**!"

> "Give it a spin!" (after the spin) "Seven hundred dollars! Pick a consonant."

> "There are two Ts. That's fourteen hundred dollars!"

> "Sorry, no Q's." / "Oh no... **Bankrupt!**"

> "You can buy a vowel for two hundred fifty dollars. Spin again, or would you like to solve?"

> "You've got it! Congratulations!"

## 🔮 Mystery Round

**What it is.** A normal round with two **Mystery wedges** on the wheel. Land on one, call a correct consonant, and you face a choice: play it safe or gamble.

**What the wheel adds.** Everything from the Normal wheel, plus two purple Mystery wedges (called Mystery 1 and Mystery 2). The top wedge here is worth **$3,500**.

### How to run it

1. Click the round button once to move from Normal to Mystery Round, then load a puzzle.
2. Spin. If the wheel lands on **Mystery 1** or **Mystery 2**, the value panel treats the spin as **$1,000 per letter**.
3. The player calls a consonant. If it is in the puzzle, they choose:
   - **Option A, play it safe:** take **$1,000 for each** matching letter. Click their round score like normal.
   - **Option B, gamble:** go to the Mystery wheel screen (click **Wheel**) and click the giant **?** circle. The wedge flips and a coin-toss-style reveal shows either **$10,000** or **Bankrupt**, 50/50.
4. Read the result:
   - **$10,000:** the value panel now says $10,000. Click **Back**, then click the player's round score. It adds $10,000 for every matching letter.
   - **Bankrupt:** the spun value clears. Click **Back** and zero the player's round score with the small **X**. The turn passes.

### Things worth knowing

- **The flipped wedge disappears.** Whichever Mystery wedge you flipped is removed from the wheel and becomes an ordinary **$700** space. The *other* Mystery wedge stays on the wheel until it is flipped too.
- **Choosing the safe $1,000 does not remove the wedge.** Only flipping does.
- **Put the wedges back** with the small blue **x** next to the Mystery result box. It also clears the reveal. (Wiping scores and puzzles restores them too.)
- If you click the **?** when the wheel is *not* sitting on a Mystery wedge, the game warns you that it is meant for flipping a Mystery wedge you just landed on, and asks whether you want to use it for another purpose anyway. Say no unless you really do.
- Bankrupt on a flip works the same as any Bankrupt, so all the money from that round is gone.

### What to say

> "You landed on a **Mystery wedge**! Pick a consonant."

> "There are two of those! Now you have a choice. Take **a thousand dollars for each**, or flip the wedge and see what's behind it. It could be **ten thousand dollars**... or it could be **Bankrupt**. What'll it be?"

> (Drum roll, then click the **?**) "And the wedge says... **TEN THOUSAND DOLLARS!**" / "...oh no. **Bankrupt.**"

## 🚂 Express Round

**What it is.** A normal round with an **Express wedge** on the wheel. Land on it, hit a letter, and you can ride the Express: keep calling letters without spinning, but one miss and it is all over.

**What the wheel adds.** Everything from the Normal wheel, plus a yellow **Express** wedge. The top wedge here is worth **$3,500**.

### How to run it

1. Click the round button until it says **Express Round**, then load a puzzle.
2. Spin. Landing on **Express** treats the spin as **$1,000 per letter**.
3. The first letter must be a **consonant**. If it is in the puzzle, reveal the tiles and click the player's round score to add $1,000 for each match.
4. The player is now **on the Express**. They may:
   - keep calling **consonants** one after another at $1,000 each, with no spinning, and
   - still **buy vowels** with the orange circle.
5. This continues until the player either **solves the puzzle** or **misses a letter**.
6. On a miss, the ride ends in **Bankrupt**. Click the small **X** next to their round score to zero it. The turn tracker passes the turn automatically.

### Things worth knowing

- The player can decide not to hop aboard. If they stop, simply click the next player's dot to hand over the turn, or have them solve.
- Each correct letter is worth $1,000 per match, and you add it with a click on the round score every time.
- If the very first consonant after landing on Express is *not* in the puzzle, the turn simply passes like in any round.
- Express is high risk and high reward: five letters in a row at $1,000 each is a big swing, and one wrong guess erases it.

### What to say

> "You landed on **Express**! Pick a consonant. It's worth a thousand dollars each."

> "Two Rs! Two thousand dollars! Now you can **hop on the Express**: keep calling letters at a thousand dollars each, but if you miss, it's **Bankrupt**. Are you riding?"

> "All aboard! ...Sorry, no M's. The Express has run out of track. **Bankrupt.**"

## 🌀 Final Spin

**What it is.** The last wheel of the main game. It has no Bankrupts, no Mystery, and no special wedges. Every player is playing for money on one big last puzzle, and the pacing is faster.

**What the wheel has.** Only dollar values (no Bankrupt, no Lose a Turn, and none of the optional wheel items). If the random spin ever lands on something that is not a dollar amount, the game quietly spins again. The top wedge is worth **$5,000**.

### The rules, as shown in the on-screen banner

> "On your turn, call any letter.
> Vowels are free, consonants will add $1,000 plus this spin.
> If your letter is in the puzzle, you get a limited time to solve."

In plain English: the player gets **one spin** for the whole round. Every consonant they call is worth **$1,000 plus the spun value**, and vowels cost nothing.

### How to run it

1. Click the round button until it says **Final Spin** (labeled *Fourth Round* on the button). A purple **FINAL SPIN** button appears on the board.
2. Click **Load Puzzle** and load the puzzle. You cannot start the Final Spin until a puzzle is loaded.
3. When you are ready, click the **FINAL SPIN** button. The screen darkens and the purple banner appears.
4. Click anywhere to spin the wheel. The sound effect and music kick in.
   - **Using a real wheel?** Click **Manually Set Final Spin Value** instead and type the amount.
5. The value panel now shows the spin **plus $1,000**. Players call letters:
   - **Vowels** are free, and there is nothing to buy. The game tells you so if someone tries.
   - **Consonants** are allowed once the spin is done. If someone tries before spinning, the game asks them to spin first.
6. If the letter is in the puzzle, the player has a limited time to solve (use the shot clock). Click the player's round score to add the value times the matches.
7. To cancel the Final Spin and go back to a regular round, click the **FINAL SPIN** button again.

### Things worth knowing

- There are no wrong-letter buzzers in this mode, and a missed letter does not clear the spun value.
- The $1,000 bonus is already folded into the number you see in the value panel, so just click the round score to add it.
- The **Transfer Totals** step works the same as any other round, so don't forget to bank the winner's money before moving on to the Bonus Round.

### What to say

> "It's time for the **Final Spin**! Here's how it works. One spin for the whole round. Every consonant you call is worth **a thousand dollars plus your spin**, and vowels are **free**. Ready? Give it a spin!"

> "Five thousand dollars! So every consonant is **six thousand**. Call a letter!"

> "There are three of those, and you've got *this* long to solve. The clock is running!"

## 💰 Bonus Round

**What it is.** The grand finale. The winner of the main game gets one puzzle, a limited set of letters, and **10 seconds** to solve it for a big prize.

### How to run it

1. Click the round button until it says **Bonus Round**. The bonus controls appear in the gray box at the top right of the board.
2. Click **Load Puzzle** and load the puzzle.
   - With **Pick-a-Category Bonus** on (the default), the game asks for **three puzzle numbers**, for example `10, 11, 12`. The contestant sees the three categories on big cards and picks one. The puzzle they pick is the one that loads.
   - Leave that box blank to load a single puzzle the usual way.
3. Click the **RSTLNE** button. The letters **R, S, T, L, N, E** are given for free and the matching tiles turn blue. Click each blue tile to reveal it.
4. The contestant now picks their own letters: **three consonants and one vowel**. Click each letter in the letter selector. They appear in the small box at the top right.
   - Holding the **Wild Card**? Then the contestant gets a **fourth consonant**.
   - If a wrong choice goes in, click the small **spiral arrow** to remove the last letter.
   - The game stops extra picks and explains why: only one vowel, and only three consonants (four with the Wild Card).
5. Click the box showing the chosen letters. All of them are revealed at once, as blue tiles. If none of them are in the puzzle, a miss sound plays.
6. Click each blue tile to reveal its letter, then click the **10** circle to start the 10-second countdown. Numbers count down, and the frame flashes when time is up. Click the circle again to reset.
7. If the contestant solves it, click the **Solve** tab to reveal the puzzle.
8. With **Bonus Wheel** on (the default), click the **Bonus Wheel** button at the top right to open the prize wheel. See below.

### The Bonus Wheel (prize envelopes)

A wheel of **24 envelopes**, with prizes from $40,000 to $100,000 plus cars by default.

1. Spin it *before* the contestant solves, so the suspense builds.
2. Click **Reveal Prize** after they solve (or when time runs out) to show what was inside.
3. Click **Edit Bonus Prizes** to change any envelope (one prize per line) or restore the defaults.
4. If a player reached the Bonus Round still holding the **Million Dollar Wedge** token, the **$100,000** envelope becomes **$1,000,000**.

The default prize mix is an approximation of the show's, not an official list. Edit it to match your game.

### What to say

> "Congratulations, you've made it to the **Bonus Round**! Your category is... you pick! Here are your three categories..."

> "I'll give you **R, S, T, L, N and E**. Now choose **three more consonants and one vowel**."

> "Let's see what you've got! ...Here we go. You have **ten seconds**. The clock starts... **now**!"

> "Time!" / "YES! You solved it! Let's see what's in your envelope..."

## 💥 Toss-Up

**What it is.** A fast, no-wheel puzzle. Letters appear one at a time and everyone races to buzz in and solve. The first correct solver wins the Toss-Up's value.

### How to run it

1. Click **Load Puzzle** and type the number followed by **T**, for example `2T`.
2. The game asks **how much the Toss-Up is worth**. The default is **$1,000**. Type a new amount or accept the default.
3. A **Toss-Up Puzzle** banner appears and the letter selector disappears. Nobody calls letters in this mode.
4. Reveal letters in one of two ways:
   - Click any blank tile to reveal that exact tile.
   - Click the **Reveal a Letter** button to reveal a random one.
   The music starts with the first reveal.
5. The first player to buzz in and solve correctly wins.
6. Click the winner's **round score**. This adds the Toss-Up value and reveals the rest of the puzzle in one click. (Clicking the **Solve** tab instead reveals the puzzle without awarding any money.)

### Things worth knowing

- If you reveal the **last remaining letter**, the game solves the puzzle for you and plays a buzz.
- With **Solve Button** set to **verify** in Settings, typing the answer is checked for you.
- The turn tracker and letter rules do not apply to Toss-Ups, so a wrong answer does not move the turn.

### What to say

> "It's time for a **Toss-Up**! The first person to solve wins a thousand dollars. Buzz in when you know it!"

> (Revealing letters one at a time) "Here comes the first letter... and another... and another..."

> "Yes! That's right! A thousand dollars to Maria!"

## 🔥 Triple Toss-Up

**What it is.** Three Toss-Ups in a row, each worth the same amount, played back to back.

### How to run it

1. Click **Load Puzzle** and type the first puzzle number followed by **TTT**. For example `3TTT` runs puzzles **3, 4 and 5**. Puzzles 4 and 5 have to exist, or the game stops you.
2. The game asks for **one value for each puzzle**. The default is **$2,000**.
3. The banner reads **Triple Toss-Up Puzzle 1**. Play it exactly like a regular Toss-Up: reveal letters, take the buzz-in, and click the winner's round score (that solves the puzzle and adds the value).
4. After puzzles 1 and 2 are solved, the left tab changes to **Load Next**. Click it to load the next puzzle. The banner moves to Puzzle 2, then Puzzle 3.
5. Round scores carry over between the three puzzles, so players build up winnings across the set.
6. After the third puzzle, use **Transfer Totals** to bank the winnings.

### Things worth knowing

- **Transfer Totals is blocked** until all three puzzles are solved. The game explains why if you try early.
- With **Special Score Bonuses** on, a player who solves **all three** gets **5 times** the Toss-Up value, for example $10,000 if each one is worth $2,000.

### What to say

> "Next up, it's the **Triple Toss-Up**! Three puzzles, two thousand dollars each. Let's play for Puzzle number one!"

> "That's one for Sam! On to Puzzle number two!"

> "Puzzle number three, the last one. Solve this and you take it all!"

## 🎁 Prize Puzzle

**What it is.** A regular puzzle, played exactly like a Normal Round puzzle, with a prize on the line for whoever solves it. A gold **PRIZE PUZZLE** ribbon appears on the board so everyone knows.

### How to set it up

1. Open **Settings**, then **Extra Features**, and click **Edit Prize Puzzle Prize**.
2. Type the prize name (for example, `A WEEKEND IN CHICAGO`) and then its dollar value.
   - The dollar value is added to the solver's round score.
   - Enter **0** to award the prize with no money attached.
3. The default prize, if you never set one, is **A TRIP TO HAWAII** worth **$10,000**.

### How to run it

1. Click **Load Puzzle** and type the number followed by **P**, for example `4P`.
2. Play it like a regular round: spin, call letters, buy vowels, solve.
3. When the puzzle is solved, a box announces the prize and asks **who solved it**. Pick the player (or **Nobody**). The prize value is added to that player's round score.
4. Bank it as usual with **Transfer Totals**.

### Things worth knowing

- Clearing the board or loading a different puzzle removes the ribbon.
- Unlike the wheel modes, the Prize Puzzle works in whichever wheel mode you are already in.

### What to say

> "This is a **Prize Puzzle**! Whoever solves it wins **a trip to Hawaii**!"

> "Congratulations, Priya, you've solved the Prize Puzzle! You're going to Hawaii!"

## 🎟️ Wheel items: Free Play, Wild Card, Gift Tag and friends

These are optional extras. Switch each one on in **Settings, then Wheel Settings** (or Extra Features for the Million Dollar Wedge). All are **off** by default.

| Item | What it does | How to use it |
|---|---|---|
| **Free Play** | The player calls a **consonant at $500** or a **vowel for free**, then gets another turn no matter what. | Land on it. Add the $500 per match for a consonant. The turn does not pass even if the letter misses. |
| **Wild Card** | Lets the player call **one more consonant** at the value of their last spin. In the Bonus Round it gives a **fourth consonant**. | Land on the item, then click the player's Wild Card token to award it. To use it, try to call a second consonant. The game offers to spend the Wild Card. |
| **Gift Tag** | A prize of your choice for the player who lands on it. | Click the player's Gift Tag token to award it. You decide and hand out the prize. |
| **Million Dollar Wedge** | A sliver of **ONE MILLION** between two Bankrupts on the Normal, Mystery and Express wheels. Landing in the middle is worth $500. | Click the green token next to the player's name to award it. If they reach the Bonus Round still holding it, the $100,000 envelope becomes $1,000,000. |
| **$5,000 Sliver** | A custom wedge with two Bankrupts and a $5,000 center. | Plays like any other wedge. |
| **$5 Wedge** | A silly custom $5 wedge. | Plays like any other wedge. |

**Claimable.** In Wheel Settings, *once* removes an item from the wheel when a player claims it, and *multiple* leaves it on the wheel for others to earn. Click **Restore wheel items** to put removed items back.

| Mode | How to enter it | Key rule | Remember to... |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Normal Round** | Default | One consonant per spin; buy vowels | Zero a player's score after Bankrupt |
| **Mystery Round** | Round button, 1 click from Normal | Mystery wedge: $1,000 each, or flip for $10,000 / Bankrupt | Use the big **?** on the wheel screen |
| **Express Round** | Round button, 2 clicks | Express wedge: ride on consonants at $1,000 each until a miss | Zero the score on a miss |
| **Final Spin** | Round button, 3 clicks | One spin; consonants are $1,000 + spin; vowels free | Load a puzzle first, then click **FINAL SPIN** |

### Handy shortcuts

- **Manual wheel:** click the Value Panel and type the amount if you are using a real wheel.
- **Turn tracker:** click any player's gold dot to hand them the turn.
- **Swap tokens:** click a player's Wild Card, Gift Tag or Million Dollar token to give or take it.
- **Skip the check:** with **Solve Button** set to verify, type `/reveal` to show the puzzle without checking.

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting

**"I can't call a consonant."** You need to spin first. A consonant can only be called after a spin, one per spin.

**"I can't call a vowel."** Buy it first with the orange circle by the player's round score, and make sure they have at least the vowel price. Right after a spin you must call a consonant before buying.

**"The Final Spin won't start."** Load a puzzle first. The game says so when you click **FINAL SPIN** with no puzzle on the board.

**"Transfer Totals does nothing in a Triple Toss-Up."** It is blocked until all three puzzles are solved. Finish the set, then bank.

**"The round button won't change."** The Bonus Round timer is running. Click the countdown circle to reset it, then switch.

**"The Mystery wedge isn't on the wheel anymore."** It was flipped. Click the small blue **x** next to the Mystery result box to restore both wedges.

**"The Prize Puzzle shows the wrong prize."** Open **Extra Features** and click **Edit Prize Puzzle Prize**.

For saving, projectors, sound and more, see the [Troubleshooting and FAQ](docs/FAQ.md). For every setting, see [Settings](docs/SETTINGS.md). To run a whole game start to finish, see [How to Use](docs/HOW-TO-USE.md).

Have fun, and good luck on the wheel! 🍀
