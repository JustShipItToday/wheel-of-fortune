# Game Modes

The round button on the puzzle board (it starts as **Normal Round**) cycles through the wheels in this order: **Normal → Mystery → Express → Final Spin → Bonus Round → Normal**. Toss-Ups, Triple Toss-Ups and Prize Puzzles are chosen when you load a puzzle.

## Normal Round
The standard game. Spin the wheel, call a consonant, and score the spun value for each match; buy vowels; solve to win the round. Wedges on the normal wheel include dollar amounts, **Bankrupt**, and **Lose a Turn**. The range of dollar values is a setting (default $500–$900).

## Toss-Up
Load a puzzle with **T** after the number (`2T`).
- You are asked how much the Toss-Up is worth (default $1,000).
- Reveal letters one at a time by clicking a blank tile, or use the **Reveal a Letter** button to reveal a random one.
- The first player to solve correctly wins the value; click their round score to add it.

## Triple Toss-Up
Load with **TTT** (`3TTT` runs puzzles 3, 4 and 5 in a row).
- You set one value for all three (default $2,000).
- The title bar shows which of the three you are on, and **Load Next** moves on.
- Transfer Totals is blocked until all three are solved; round scores are kept between them.
- With **Special Score Bonuses** on, a player who solves all three gets **5×** the Toss-Up value (for example $10,000 if each is worth $2,000).

## Mystery Round
The Mystery wheel has two **Mystery wedges**. If a player lands on one and calls a correct letter, they choose:
1. **$1,000 for each** matching letter, or
2. **Gamble**: click the giant **?** on the Mystery screen to flip the wedge, which reveals **$10,000** or **Bankrupt** at random.

That Mystery wedge disappears, and the other becomes a standard $1,000 space.

## Express Round
The Express wheel has an **Express wedge**. If a player lands on it and calls a correct letter (worth $1,000 each), they may **hop aboard the Express**: they keep calling letters at $1,000 each, but an incorrect letter means **Bankrupt**. High risk, high reward.

## Final Spin
The last wheel of the main game; all wheel items are disabled. Rounds continue on this wheel until the host starts the **Final Spin** with the Final Spin button on the board, then follows the banner:
- On your turn, call any letter.
- Vowels are free; consonants add $1,000 plus the spin.
- If the letter is in the puzzle, the player has limited time to solve.

## Bonus Round
Played by the winner of the main game.
- Give the contestant **R, S, T, L, N, E** with the RSTLNE box.
- They choose **three more consonants and a vowel** (a fourth consonant if they hold the Wild Card).
- A **10-second** countdown runs for the solve.
- With **Pick-a-Category Bonus** on, you enter three puzzle numbers and the contestant picks one of the three categories first.
- With **Bonus Wheel** on, spin the 24-envelope wheel for the prize. See [Features](FEATURES.md).

## Prize Puzzle *(new in this edition)*
Load with **P** (`4P`). The first time, you name the prize and its dollar value. When the puzzle is solved, you choose who solved it, and the prize value is added to **that player's round score**. Enter $0 to award the prize without adding money.

## Wheel items

| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Free Play** | The player calls a consonant at $500 or a vowel for free, then gets another turn regardless of the result. Off by default. |
| **Wild Card** | When used, lets the player call another consonant worth the value of their prior spin. In the Bonus Round it gives an extra consonant. Off by default. |
| **Gift Tag** | An item that gives the player a prize of your choice. Off by default. |
| **Million Dollar Wedge** | See [Features](FEATURES.md). Off by default. |
| **$5,000 Sliver** | A custom wedge split into two Bankrupts and a $5,000 center. Off by default. |
| **$5 Wedge** | A custom $5 wedge. Off by default. |

Wheel items are removed from the wheel when claimed (or stay, if you set Claimable to *multiple*). The **Restore wheel items** button puts them back.
